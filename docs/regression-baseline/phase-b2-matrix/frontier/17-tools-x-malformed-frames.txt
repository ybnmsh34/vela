GATE M Part 1 (Phase B2) — profile: frontier — case 17-tools-x-malformed-frames: junk frames woven through a batch of parallel calls
================================================================================

PURPOSE   MEASURED-2 says skip a bad frame and keep going. MEASURED-4 says `index` is the only join key, and it lives in the frames. Individually right, jointly dangerous: a skipped frame is exactly what moves a fragment into the wrong call.
SUBJECT   all three shipping adapters
ENDPOINT  one purpose-built loopback peer per adapter, real TCP
HONESTY   VERIFIED-BY-FAKE. The endpoint is a deterministic mock; no model produced
          any byte below. Nothing here is evidence about a real model.

  FOUR KINDS OF JUNK, woven between the fragments of three real calls:
    1. a frame that is not JSON at all
    2. an empty `data:` line
    3. a line that is not an SSE field at all
    4. A TRUNCATED FRAME THAT IS THE PREFIX OF A REAL CALL
  The fourth is the one that matters. A consumer that recovers optimistically turns
  it into a phantom fourth call; one that resynchronises badly loses the real third.
  THE NON-STREAMED ARM drives the other half of FINDING 1's shape: a whole batch of
  three in which only the MIDDLE one is broken. A consumer that loses calls reports
  one where the socket carried three.
