== 9b. CORRECTION — section 9 is INCONCLUSIVE, do not read it as a pass ==
  Section 9 reported "no Print dialog process was found" after sending Ctrl+P.
  That conclusion is UNSAFE and is withdrawn.

  On re-test with the foreground window verified at send time, the foreground
  was 'Windows Input Experience' (textinputhost), not 'Vela'. Ten attempts to
  force Vela to the foreground failed. So the Ctrl+P/Ctrl+F/Ctrl+B keystrokes
  in section 9 may never have reached the app at all, and the absence of a
  Print dialog proves nothing.

  Whether WebView2's native Ctrl+P (print) and Ctrl+F (find-in-page) are
  suppressed in Vela is therefore UNVERIFIED. It cannot be settled with CDP
  Input.dispatchKeyEvent either, because that dispatches into the page and
  never exercises the browser's own accelerator handling — real OS input is
  required, and this machine's IME host is intercepting it.

  Note the earlier Ctrl+K and the section 7c activation test DID reach the app
  (the app demonstrably responded), so foreground capture is intermittent
  rather than total. Those results stand; section 9 does not.
