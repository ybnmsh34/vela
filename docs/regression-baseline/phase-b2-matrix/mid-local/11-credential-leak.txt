---- ROUND 4 — the ESCAPING peers, whose bytes contain no literal canary at all 
  These two peers percent-decode the `key` parameter and echo the raw credential
  back with `/` written `\/`, which is what PHP's `json_encode` does by default.
  The assertion below is the PREMISE of the round-4 cases and it is the opposite of
  the one above: the canary must NOT appear literally in what these peers wrote, or
  a byte-literal scrub would have caught it and the cases would prove nothing. What
  must appear is the ESCAPED spelling — the one Vela's own decoder turns back into
  a credential.
