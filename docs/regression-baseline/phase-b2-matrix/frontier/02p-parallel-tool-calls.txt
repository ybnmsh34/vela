  WHY THIS CASE EXISTS
  Round 1 could only SCRIPT this shape: the harness answered every tool request with
  exactly one call, and one call cannot collide with itself. That blindness is why the
  defect reached a gate. Three tools are offered below, so the endpoint answers with a
  BATCH — native profiles well-formed, hostile with the middle call broken — and every
  byte below came off a real socket from the profile's own OS process.

  The two shapes are NOT the same shape, and that is the whole point:
  streamed      `delta.tool_calls[]`   — FRAGMENTS, joined by `index`
  non-streamed  `message.tool_calls[]` — WHOLE CALLS, and no `index` exists
  The assertions below therefore compare identity/name/arguments/reason, and record
  the `index` difference as the legitimate wire difference it is.
